Where the
moment

In the cobblestoned heart of Vecrīga, OLYV brings together Mediterranean-inspired dining, the warmth of the grill and the simple pleasure of sharing something delicious.

Settle in for a leisurely lunch, meet friends over cocktails, or make an evening of it with music and conversation. Inside or out on the terrace, every visit is an invitation to enjoy the moment.
